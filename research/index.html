Research

Currently, my research has been focused on where applied physics, materials science/engineering, and electrical engineering intersect.

I worked with Dr. Feng Jin in his Nanomaterials and Devices Laboratory at Ball State University a little longer than a year. The experience was incredibly valuable and exposed me to this subfield that I had no previous experience with.

My brain has a strong desire to understand the fundamental principles at work in any situation, so this was a good outlet for that curiosity.

Since I value having a diverse experience, I am interested in other research areas that are in this also technical intersection.
